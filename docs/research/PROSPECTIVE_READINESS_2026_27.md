## Three states, deliberately

| state | meaning |
|---|---|
| `READY` | wired, scheduled, **and observed writing real rows** |
| `ARMED` | wired and scheduled, but has never written a row — correct-looking, unproven |
| `NOT_READY` | a required piece is missing; nothing will be collected |

`ARMED` exists because an offseason no-op and a silent failure look identical from the outside. Reporting an
armed stream as ready is the failure this audit is meant to prevent: **a horizon with no snapshot is never
reported as a covered horizon, and a stream that has never run is never reported as a working one.**

"Reachable by policy" is arithmetic on the cadence — the tier covering each horizon ticks at least as often as
the 12-minute freshness threshold. It is **not** a claim that a snapshot exists. **Observed coverage is zero
for every horizon**, because no 2026-27 game has yet been inside the capture window. That is reported as zero,
not as success.

Two reporting defects were found and fixed:

1. **T-60m was not reported.** `HORIZONS_MINUTES` covered 24h, 6h, 90m, 30m and 10m. The cadence already
   produced a snapshot near T-60m, but a horizon that is collected and never reported is a horizon nobody can
   show was collected. T-60m now appears alongside the rest.
2. **An unmeasured window reported `FAIL`.** With no games in the window, `pct_within_12min` is `null` and the
   acceptance verdict read `FAIL`. That conflates *not measured* with *measured and failed*. `PASS` would have
   been the error the brief names outright; `FAIL` is the quieter version of the same mistake, because an
   alarm that is red for four months every summer is one people learn to scroll past — and it is red again on
   the night it matters. There is now a third verdict, `NO_DATA`, carrying the reason. A game that *exists*
   and has no snapshots is still a real `FAIL`; a test pins that distinction.

## Information events

`matchup/events.py` defines five event kinds: `lineup_confirmed`, `starter_change`, `late_scratch`,
`rotation_addition`, `assignment_shift`. `stratify()` needs **two** `matchup/context` snapshots for a game
before it can emit a single event.

**There are zero `matchup/context` rows in the archive** — against 39,807 `kalshi/markets` and 21,335
`context/rosters`. Every stratum, `no_event` included, is therefore empty, and the Phase 7 stratification
result is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than "no effect". This is measured from the archive manifest, not assumed.

`matchup-shadow` is gated on the same condition as `context` and writes nothing while no game falls inside its
36-hour horizon, which is the documented offseason behaviour and matches the worker's 120-hours-to-tip. It is
`ARMED`, not `READY`: it has never written a row in production, so the correct no-op and a silent failure
cannot yet be told apart. The first snapshots are expected around **2026-10-02**, and the first *event*
requires a second snapshot of the same game after that.

### The confirmed-starter blocker

This is an **absent source**, not a discarded field, and the code already refuses to paper over it.
`shadow.build_context` leaves `expected_home_starters` / `expected_away_starters` empty on purpose:

> Deliberately empty: no source reports expected starters today, and a projection written here would be
> indistinguishable from a confirmation later.

That is the right call and should stay. The only starter flag anywhere in the repo is on the **box score**,
which is post-game and would be leakage for a pregame forecast — exactly what `matchup/leakage.py` exists to
prevent.

Consequences, stated plainly:

- `LINEUP_CONFIRMED` and `STARTER_CHANGE` **cannot fire at all**.
- `lineup_confidence` stays `UNKNOWN` on every context.
- `LATE_SCRATCH` and `ROTATION_ADDITION` still work — they diff the captured roster, which *is* ingested.

**This needs a source, not a code change.** It is out of scope for this wave, which was asked to verify
readiness rather than build another stream, and it is recorded here as the top blocker for the next one.

## Horizon completeness reporting

Each horizon is now graded `COVERED` / `STALE` / `MISSING` rather than carrying a bare timestamp, and
everything that is not `COVERED` carries a reason.

`COVERED` requires all three of: a real snapshot at or before the target, taken **inside that game's capture
window**, and no older than the 12-minute tolerance. Nothing else can reach it. In particular a snapshot from
before the window — yesterday's slate — does not cover tonight's T-30m, and a snapshot forty minutes stale does
not cover T-30m merely by being the nearest one on record; that is `STALE`, reported with its age.

Missing reasons are limited to what timestamps can actually establish:

| reason | when |
|---|---|
| `no market snapshot exists on the archive at all` | archive empty |
| `capture had not started when this horizon passed` | target precedes the first snapshot |
| `no snapshot was taken inside this game's capture window` | archive spans the horizon, nothing landed in-window |
| `no snapshot at or before this horizon, and the timestamps do not say why` | unexplained — never guessed |

Causes that need payload rather than timing — *market not listed yet*, *quote not executable*, *model stale*,
*context stale* — are deliberately **not** inferred here. Asserting them from capture times would be a guess
wearing a reason's clothing. They are reported by the readiness audit against the streams that carry them, and
a horizon whose cause cannot be established stays `UNEXPLAINED` rather than being assigned a plausible one.

The aggregate now prints `n_covered + n_stale + n_missing` against `n_games` per horizon, so a horizon whose
games do not add up is visible as a reporting bug instead of being silently absorbed.

Four mutations were introduced against these rules and all four were caught: a stale snapshot counting as
covered, a pre-window snapshot counting as covered, missing horizons carrying no reason, and every missing
cause collapsing to a single reason.

Three of these are newly pinned by tests rather than only inspected:

- **Matchup shadow cannot abort market capture.** It runs *before* market capture in the same job, so without
  `continue-on-error` a bad research pull would take the night's market snapshots with it. Market capture is
  the one stream that cannot be re-collected later: a price at T-30m exists for thirty minutes and never
  again.
- **Collection and reporting steps have opposite requirements.** Every collection step must be isolated; the
  alarm step must *not* be, or a silent coverage failure ends as a green run — which is how ~4.6% delivery
  went unnoticed for a whole wave. The test asserts both directions.
- **The 2026-27 rollover stays in season.** `in_season` does not blink off between the preseason finale
  (2026-10-18) and opening night (2026-10-20); a dormant day there is market history that cannot be
  recovered. 2026-10-02, the day before preseason opens, is correctly dormant *and* correctly not on the
  October–June fallback.

One risk this wave introduced was checked directly and is clear: the new default research population could
have starved a live consumer during 2026-27 preseason. `load_shot_events` has **no live consumer** — only
`scripts/build_shot_profile_features.py` and tests — and nothing under `sim/`, `pricing/` or `workflows/`
imports `shotprofile` at all. The filter cannot reach production behaviour.
