# §5.14 — Kalshi dollar-quote handling in `simulate` (corrected, NOT activated)

## Defect
`workflows/simulate.py` read bare `yes_bid/yes_ask/no_bid/no_ask` from board rows. Kalshi boards carry `*_dollars` fields (and the capture-time
`_quote_cents`), so every archived prediction (444,322) has `p_market`, `p_hybrid`, EV, best side and `market_*` quotes = null, and every
supported contract is `NO_EDGE`/`no_quote`. Market-relative research from the ledger is impossible.

## Change
- `market_snapshot(m)`: builds the cents snapshot from `_quote_cents` (preferred, degenerate 0/100 already removed) else a fresh normalisation of the row
  (`*_dollars` or legacy cents). Feeds `p_market` (mid of yes bid/ask, else last), `p_hybrid`, `compute_economics` (EV, best side, bet-up-to, spread, staleness),
  the frozen `market_*` prediction fields and the slate rows/markdown. Missing sides stay `None`; nothing is invented.
- **Inert on merge:** gated by `NBA_EDGE_QUOTE_FIX=1`. Unset (the default, and what every workflow uses) keeps the pre-fix behaviour byte-for-byte via
  `legacy_market_snapshot`. No workflow sets the flag. Activation is a separate, explicit decision.
- Tests: `tests/test_simulate_quotes.py` (dollar-only/`_quote_cents`/legacy/no-quote rows; economics; end-to-end dollar board == cent board with the flag on;
  default run unchanged with the flag off). The end-to-end equivalence test fails on the pre-fix reader.

## ⚠ Do not activate yet: the corrected quotes expose model-input defects
Median |p_data − p_market|: game scope 0.082 (7.9% of contracts differ by >0.25), **player scope 0.110 (22.7% differ by >0.25)**. 1,523 of the 3,861
new "recommendations" disagree with the market by more than 25 points. The largest ones are not edges, they are broken player inputs, e.g. Giannis
Antetokounmpo 25+ points: `p_data` 0.00035 vs market 0.67 (packet: `games_used`=4, `fga_per_min`=0.126, simulated mean 2.9 points at 21 projected minutes;
negative ESPN-derived `nba_id`; 2026-27 team change not reflected in history). That is the preseason-prior / ID-mismatch / thin-history class of issue tracked under item 7.
Activating the corrected quotes today would convert those model failures into `Gate.OK` "buy NO" lines.

Recommended before activation (needs your decision): (1) land the item-7 input fixes for player usage/minutes priors; (2) add a model-vs-market divergence gate
(e.g. refuse OK when |p_data − p_market| exceeds a preregistered bound for player scope); (3) shadow-run with the flag on and compare to realised outcomes
for ≥ the preregistered 40 games / 200 independent decisions before any recommendation is shown.

No change to live prediction parameters, strategy thresholds, stakes, exposure limits, execution, the frozen baseline, the control, or t60a.
