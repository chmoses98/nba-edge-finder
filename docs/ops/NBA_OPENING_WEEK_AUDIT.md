## 3. Missing or broken (found)

1. **Scheduling defect (critical, fixed in PR #33).** `projection-foundation.yml` decided what to run from the wall-clock ET hour at the moment a *delayed* schedule event executed. Runs 11 (06:15Z; a 21:20 ET slot delivered ~5 h late) and 13 (17:08Z; 13:08 ET) both concluded `success` with `live` and `sweep` skipped. GitHub schedule delivery on this repository is 1.5-5 h late, so all pregame slots were at risk.
2. The incumbent sweep cron started at 21:50Z, after the 19:00Z opener's T-60.
3. No run ledger or watchdog: a green workflow carried no information about whether projections were generated.
4. No game-level (team points) forecast archive, no team outcome archive (final points, quarter scores, possessions), no market-snapshot archive tied to forecasts.
5. No paper-trading engine, no expression ranking, no daily scorecards against market prices, no weekly report.
6. Market-family coverage: the ontology reports 1,642 UNRESOLVED markets (novelty/futures/off-court series: awards, franchise, executive, etc.). They are correctly not treated as basketball opportunities; this stays a documented alarm, not a defect.
